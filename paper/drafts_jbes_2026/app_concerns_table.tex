\subsection{Design concerns and where each is addressed}
\label{app:concerns}

A text-as-treatment estimate read under spatial confounding invites a standard set
of concerns, most of them raised in the methodological literatures the paper draws
on. Table~\ref{tab:concerns} lists them, names the work that articulated each, and
points to the analysis in this paper that meets it. The table is a reading guide
rather than a new result, and every row is developed at length in the section it
references.

Concern & Raised by & Addressed in this paper \\

The text treatment aliases the other latent features the same prose carries, so no
separated semantic effect is identified &
\citet{fong2023latent} &
The estimand is stated as the price effect of the leading text direction
marginalizing over whatever else the language encodes, an associational object
rather than a separated attribute (Section~\ref{sec:decomposition}). \\

The text effect is spatial information re-expressed, priced for the geography it
encodes &
\citet{gilbert2021causal} &
The erasure diagnostic confirms the exposure retains non-spatial variation, and the
deconfounding ladder holds location fixed by construction rather than by adjustment
(Sections~\ref{sec:leace-12},~\ref{sec:decomposition}). \\

The scale at which location is controlled is an unpinned modeling choice &
\citet{keller2020selecting} &
The control basis spans ZIP indicators and a quadratic in coordinates, and the
identified direction moves little across the basis, attenuating only where the
leading component is most geographic (Section~\ref{sec:identified-direction}). \\

Location and non-spatial signal can be separated by design rather than assumed apart &
\citet{guan2023spectral} &
The identified direction is the embedding analogue of spectral separation, projecting
the location-predictable subspace out of the text before estimation
(Section~\ref{sec:identified-direction}). \\

A treatment learned from the documents, formed on the same data as the outcome,
leaves the influence-function interval anticonservative &
\citet{egami2022texts} &
Forming the direction on the pooled corpus shrinks the generated-regressor channel,
an out-of-fold projection removes most of what remains, and a full-pipeline bootstrap
carries the residual into the reported intervals (Appendix~\ref{app:simulation}). \\

The estimate could depend on which machine learner fits the nuisances &
\citet{chernozhukov2018double} &
The cross-fitted Neyman-orthogonal score admits any learner converging faster than
$n^{-1/4}$; the ridge headline and its gradient-boosted counterpart return the same
per-market pattern and New York reversal (Appendix~\ref{app:simulation}). \\

Concept erasure removes only the linear part of the encoded location &
\citet{belrose2023leace} &
Linear erasure is exact with a guardedness guarantee in every market, and the
nonlinear residual a linear probe cannot see is bounded separately by iterative
gradient-based projection (Section~\ref{sec:leace-12}). \\

The prose carries omitted categorical structure the controls cannot represent, such
as property type &
\citet{roberts2020adjusting} &
A composition-and-conditioning audit flags the vacant-land case and adjusts for it by
exact within-category demeaning, which does not explain the effect away in any market
(Section~\ref{sec:coca}). \\

An unobserved confounder, unmeasured condition among them, drives the result &
\citet{cinelli2020making} &
A per-market robustness value is benchmarked against the observed spatial block and
against a measured assessor quality grade where one exists, neither of which
overturns the estimate (Section~\ref{sec:sensitivity-12}). \\

Predictive accuracy from listing text may be redundant spatial encoding rather than
new signal &
\citet{nowak2017textual} &
The erasure diagnostic and the decomposition separate the geography the representation
merely encodes from the geography that moves the price, which is what distinguishes
priced content from a linguistic proxy (Section~\ref{sec:decomposition}). \\
