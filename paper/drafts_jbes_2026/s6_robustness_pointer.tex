
\subsection{Robustness}
\label{sec:robustness-body}

The effect withstands the challenges a skeptic would raise, each developed in the
supplement. A sensitivity analysis reports how strong an unobserved confounder would
have to be to overturn a market's estimate, a threshold no observed covariate
reaches in any of the twelve (Appendix~\ref{sec:sensitivity-12}). The two text
encodings, a Doc2Vec uniqueness axis and the sentence-BERT component, move together
across the panel, so the surviving effect is not an artifact of one representation
(Appendix~\ref{sec:cross-method-12}). Re-estimated across ten markets on the price a
property actually fetches rather than the one its agent posts, it replicates on
recorded transactions and is individually significant in every one
(Appendix~\ref{sec:saleprice}). And a counterfactual rewriting intervention, which
holds the model's inferred location fixed while stripping stylistic content,
isolates a positive style effect of the expected sign
(Appendix~\ref{sec:counterfactual-12}). These analyses qualify the ladder rather
than extend it.
