# Appendix: residual non-linear leakage after LEACE deconfounding

The closed-form LEACE projection of Belrose et al. (NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2306.03819) drives the cross-covariance Sigma_{X,Z} to machine zero in one pass and satisfies the Theorem 1 linear-guardedness identity P @ Sigma_{X,Z} = 0 to 1e-16 in all three cities (SF, NYC, Boston). The headline diagnostic of the deconfounding is therefore not in dispute. The diagnostic that an attentive reader (or a JBES referee) will press is whether a non-linear residual survives. On the San Francisco subsample, an MLP probe recovers a held-out R^2 of 0.086 on standardised latitude and longitude from the LEACE-erased embeddings, down from 0.124 on the raw embeddings but appreciably above the comparator cities, which sit at -0.28 (NYC) and -0.03 (Boston). Two competing readings are admissible. Under the first, the 0.086 is a finite-sample artefact of the kind Kumar, Tan and Sharma (NeurIPS 2022, arXiv:2207.04153) warn against, in which case the SF deconfounding is in fact complete. Under the second, it is a genuine non-linear residual that survives linear erasure, in which case the SF claim of full deconfounding cannot be made on the LEACE-only embeddings. We adjudicate this with a row-permutation null: shuffling the LEACE-erased rows row-wise and re-fitting the same MLP returns R^2 values centred at -1.36 with standard deviation 0.25 across five permutations, placing the observed 0.086 at 5.77 sigma above the null. We therefore treat the residual as real and apply Iskander, Radinsky and Belinkov's Iterative Gradient-Based Projection (Findings of ACL 2023, arXiv:2305.10204), iterated five times against an MLP adversary, as a non-linear polish on top of LEACE. The polish reduces SF held-out MLP R^2(lat,lon) to 0.005 while preserving Ridge R^2 on log price to within 0.4 percentage points, and preserves the LEACE linear-guardedness identity exactly because every IGBP step is a right-multiplication by an orthogonal projector. Re-running the LEACE-only diagnostic up to ten iterations on the same layer is by contrast a no-op, as the cross-covariance is already at 5e-16 after iteration one. We report headline DML estimates on the LEACE+IGBP embeddings and treat the LEACE-only specification as a sensitivity check.
